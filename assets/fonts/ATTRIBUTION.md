# Font attribution

This app bundles the **Cairo** typeface (Regular, Medium, SemiBold, Bold,
ExtraBold) for its Arabic-first UI.

- Font: Cairo, designed by Mohamed Gaber and Accademia di Belle Arti di Urbino
  students (Guglielmo Lescano, et al.)
- Source: Google Fonts (https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Cairo)
